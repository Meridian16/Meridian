Important service-area-business note:
No street address or geo coordinates were added because the current site does not publish a physical customer-facing business address. Google Business Profile guidance allows service-area businesses that do not serve customers at their address to hide that address. Google Search's LocalBusiness rich-result documentation, however, currently lists a physical address as a required property for LocalBusiness rich-result eligibility. This markup remains useful Schema.org business/service context, but a LocalBusiness rich result is not guaranteed without a compliant physical address. Do not add a made-up, virtual, mailbox, or hidden address solely for structured data.
